# Dune Strike governance

Dune Strike is a small, maintainer-led open-source project. This document
describes how decisions are made so contributors know what to expect. It is not
a legal entity and creates no binding obligations.

## Maintainers

- Maintainers review pull requests, merge changes, cut releases and keep the
  issue tracker healthy.
- A maintainer may be added by agreement of the existing maintainers after a
  sustained record of useful contributions.
- A maintainer may step down at any time, or be removed for inactivity or
  repeated violations of the code of conduct.

## Decisions

- Routine changes are decided by the maintainer reviewing the change.
- Larger changes — protocol changes, new dependencies, rebrands, releases and
  changes to this document — need agreement from at least two maintainers when
  more than one is active.
- When there is disagreement, the change waits until a maintainer writes down
  the trade-offs in the pull request or issue.

## Releases

- Releases are cut from `main` after the CI build passes on Linux, Windows and
  macOS.
- Release notes list user-visible changes and any compatibility notes.
- Security fixes may be released without a long review period.

## Contributions

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). By contributing you agree that your
contribution can be distributed under the project's zlib-style license.

## Conduct

Be civil. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not welcome.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block accounts that make the
project hostile.
