# Credits, origin and licensing

Dune Strike is an altered, rebranded distribution of the **AssaultCube**
codebase, which itself is built on the **Cube engine**. The original code,
assets and tools remain the work of their respective authors. This file exists
so the origin is not misrepresented, as required by the zlib-style license and
the asset licenses.

## Engine and original game

- **Cube engine** — Wouter "Aardappel" van Oortmerssen, Lee "Eihrul" Salzman
  and contributors. <http://cubeengine.com>
- **AssaultCube** — an international community of artists and developers
  (2004–present). The original project, credits and licensing information are
  preserved in `source/README.txt`, `source/README_CUBEENGINE.txt`, the in-game
  **About** menu and the per-package `license.txt` files.
- **pCube (Cube pro mod)** — Adrian "driAn" Henke and contributors.
- **Sauerbraten (Cube 2)** — Wouter van Oortmerssen, Lee Salzman, Mike "Gilt"
  Dysart and contributors.

## Technology

- **ENet** networking library — Lee Salzman and contributors.
- **SDL2, SDL2_image, OpenAL, Ogg Vorbis, zlib** — their respective authors.
- **GL4ES** OpenGL translation — ptitSeb and contributors.

## Art, audio, maps and community

The full contributor lists for art, audio, textures, maps, platform work and
community support are kept in the in-game **About** menu and in
`docs/package_copyrights.txt`. Individual packages also carry their own
`license.txt` files; those notices must be kept when redistributing the
corresponding assets.

## New work in this distribution

The Dune Strike name, the new loading screen and icon, the hijabi player skins,
the push-to-talk voice chat implementation and the release/build tooling were
added for this distribution. They do not claim authorship of the original
engine, game code or assets.
